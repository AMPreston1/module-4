Carolina Hurricanes Game

Going to a Carolina Hurricanes game is an experience that is a must in the Carolinas! The energy in the arena is electric and the fans are passionate about their team. It is considered to be one of the best experiences in hockey and is the loudest arena in the NHL! The fan base is like family and the team and the fans a community that is one of the best in all of sports. If you want a fun time and an experience with great people and great hockey, then you need to go to a Carolina Hurricanes game!

To Do List for a Carolina Hurricanes Game

  • Tailgate
  • Go to the Team Store
  • Have some Barbeque
  • Meet the Mascot, Stormy
  • Experience a Storm Surge After a Win
  • Meet Players after the game for autographs
